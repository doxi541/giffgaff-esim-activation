# giffgaff eSIM: how do you activate it, how long does it take, and where do people get stuck? (Compatibility, the 04:30–21:30 switch window and going back to a plastic SIM)

A giffgaff eSIM is activated inside the giffgaff app and nowhere else. There is no QR code, no SM-DP+ address and no manual activation code, so a QR sticker in a shop window or a screenshot from a friend is no use here. Existing members moving off a physical SIM or an older eSIM usually see service in under an hour, with up to 24 hours at busy times, and the whole switch has to happen between 04:30 and 21:30 UK time.

Below is the activation route for new and existing customers, which handsets actually work, the three places people lose an afternoon, and how to get back onto a plastic SIM if the eSIM route goes wrong. giffgaff runs on O2's network, so coverage is the same footprint you'd get from any O2-based provider.

> **Quick version.** Joining giffgaff? Install the app, create an account, flip the dashboard toggle to eSIM, buy a plan, install. Already a member? App → **Account → SIM → Replace my SIM → Switch to a new eSIM**. Start inside the app before anything else.

## How to get a giffgaff eSIM as a new customer

1. Check the handset supports eSIM (table further down). This is a hardware feature; no software update adds it later.
2. Download the latest giffgaff app and register a new account.
3. On the app dashboard, switch the toggle to eSIM, then choose and pay for a plan.
4. Install the eSIM when prompted and follow the on-screen steps.
5. If you're porting a number from another network, do it after the eSIM is up and running. giffgaff lets you bring your number across once the eSIM is active.

If you start the order on the website instead, you'll still be pushed into the app to finish activation. The plan only goes live on your account once that app-based activation completes.

**On an eSIM-only phone**, the setup wizard will offer to activate an eSIM for you. Skip that step. giffgaff doesn't support the manufacturer's built-in activation flow yet. Get through the phone setup without a line, install the app, sign in, then activate from the dashboard.

Two practical requirements: the activation has to be completed on the device that will use the eSIM, and you want a stable connection while doing it, with Wi-Fi recommended. If your phone dies or the connection drops mid-install, reopen the app and pick up where you left off.

## Already with giffgaff? The switch route

1. Log into the giffgaff app first. giffgaff stresses this step because the switch is initiated from inside your account.
2. Go to **Account → SIM → Replace my SIM → Switch to a new eSIM**.
3. Follow the prompts to install and activate.
4. Enter the SMS verification code sent to your current giffgaff number.
5. Restart the phone if prompted.

Timing splits by customer type, and the distinction matters if you're planning around it. New members should see the eSIM activate as soon as it's installed; if nothing connects, restart or flick flight mode on and off for a soft reset. Existing members switching from a physical SIM or an old eSIM should wait under an hour, and up to 24 hours during busy periods. If there's still no signal after an hour, restart the phone at 30-minute intervals. Past 24 hours, contact an agent rather than continuing to fiddle.

## Which phones work with a giffgaff eSIM?

Three conditions have to be met together: the device supports eSIM, it's network unlocked, and it can run the latest version of the giffgaff app.

| Manufacturer | Works with giffgaff eSIM |
| --- | --- |
| Apple | iPhone XR and XS (2018) onwards. iPhone 13 and newer can run two eSIMs at once |
| Samsung | Galaxy S20 onwards, A23 onwards, plus Z Fold, Z Flip and Note 20 |
| Google Pixel | Pixel 4 onwards. Pixel 3, 3 XL, 3a and 3a XL work only on UK versions; models sold in Japan and some other regions don't |
| Others | Oppo Find X3/X5/N2, Honor Magic4 Pro 5G and Magic5 Pro, Sony Xperia 10 IV, Xiaomi Mi 12T Pro |

Outside Apple, Samsung and Google, compatibility gets patchier, and the manufacturer's own spec sheet is the reliable check. A community report from a Samsung Galaxy M23 owner is a fair warning here: the phone has two SIM slots and a microSD slot, but no option anywhere in settings to enable an eSIM. Mid-range and budget models are where the surprises live, even within a brand that supports eSIM elsewhere in its range.

Wearables are out. giffgaff doesn't support eSIM in smartwatches and gives no timeframe, citing missing network-side components. iPads work only on recent models, and again it's a hardware feature baked in at manufacture.

## Where people get stuck

### The 04:30–21:30 window

Adding, switching or replacing an eSIM is only possible between 04:30 and 21:30 UK time. Try it at 11pm and the option simply won't complete. If you're ordering from outside the UK, convert the window before you sit down with the app, because a failed attempt at the wrong hour looks identical to a broken eSIM.

### The old SIM that refuses to leave

One number carries one active SIM. The moment the new eSIM activates, the old physical SIM or old eSIM stops working, and leaving it in the phone can interfere with the new one. Take the plastic SIM out, or delete the old eSIM from settings, being careful not to delete the new profile in the process.

### The SMS verification deadlock

Switching to a replacement eSIM as an existing member requires a one-time code sent by text to your current giffgaff number. If your old eSIM is broken and can't receive texts, you're locked out of the very screen that would fix it. giffgaff's workaround is to go back to plastic first:

1. Get a brand-new, unactivated giffgaff SIM. Order one free from giffgaff, or buy one for around £1 from Tesco, Sainsbury's, ASDA, Morrisons, Co-Op, Iceland, Home Bargains, B&M, Poundland, One Below, WH Smith or McColl's.
2. Log into your giffgaff account before doing anything else, via the app or the website dashboard.
3. Go to **Replace my SIM** and enter the 13-digit activation code printed on the back of the new card.
4. Choose "I'm registered with giffgaff" and sign in. No SMS code needed, and your number, plan and credit transfer automatically.
5. Once the physical SIM is working, wait at least 24 hours before switching back to an eSIM.

That 24-hour wait isn't padding. It's the instruction, and rushing it tends to produce a second failed switch.

## Switching back to a physical SIM

You'll need a brand-new, unactivated giffgaff SIM; a old one you've used before won't work. Log into the app or website, then choose **Account → SIM → Replace my SIM → Switch to a new physical SIM** and follow the prompts. Expect the same 04:30–21:30 window to apply.

giffgaff also runs a recycling scheme for old handsets and unused SIM cards if you end up with spares in a drawer.

## FAQ

**Can I use a QR code from somewhere else to set up a giffgaff eSIM?**
No. giffgaff doesn't issue QR codes, SM-DP+ addresses or manual activation codes, and the app is the only activation channel. Quick Transfer between phones isn't supported either.

**My phone says "Unable to activate eSIM" during setup. Is it broken?**
Not necessarily. If you followed the correct steps for your account type and the settings look right, giffgaff says the message can be ignored and the eSIM should still work. Verify it's switched on: on iOS, **Settings → Mobile Data → SIMs**, tap the eSIM and check **Turn On This Line**; on Android, **Settings → SIM cards and mobile networks → Manage eSIM**.

**The eSIM shows as connected but calls, texts and data don't work. What now?**
This usually follows moving an eSIM to a new phone. Work through it in order: confirm the giffgaff eSIM is set as default for calls, texts and data (on a dual-SIM phone another line may be grabbing it); toggle flight mode on for 10 seconds and off; remove or disable any other SIMs; restart fully; then test the eSIM in your old phone to see whether the fault is the SIM or the new handset.

**How many eSIMs can one phone hold?**
Some handsets store eight or more eSIM profiles, with one or two active at a time. Your giffgaff account is stricter: only one SIM or eSIM per number can be active, regardless of what the phone can hold.

**Can I keep my number?**
Yes. Port your number after the eSIM is installed and working. Grab a PAC code by texting PAC to 65075 and use it within 30 days.
